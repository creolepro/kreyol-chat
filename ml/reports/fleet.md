## Part 2 — the questions

### Q1 — Tokenizer — does the Kreyòl vocabulary improve LEARNING (BPB), not just cost?

*Setup: tokenizer (kreyol-bpe vs english-24k); identical v0.2.1 data/order/steps. 2 seed(s). Decides: Station 1's tokenizer-thesis claim strength (causal, not just fertility).*

| condition | general ↓ | authored ↓ | transl. ↓ | authored_v2 ↓ | FLORES ↓ |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| kreyol-bpe | 1.8409 ±0.071 | 2.1987 ±0.081 | 1.7944 ±0.060 | 1.8828 ±0.068 | 2.0834 ±0.035 |
| english-24k | 1.9425 ±0.041 | 2.2973 ±0.061 | 1.8821 ±0.035 | 1.9886 ±0.049 | 2.1704 ±0.082 |

**Answer.** Yes — at identical v0.2.1 data/order/compute the Kreyòl vocabulary reaches **0.102 bits/byte lower** general BPB than the English-24k ablation (same size, pattern, algorithm), so the vocabulary improves LEARNING, not just cost.

**Decision.** Station 1 can claim the tokenizer causally — kreyol-bpe is confirmed for G-v1.

### Q2 — Mix weights — does authored-upweighting shift the model's voice?

*Setup: sampling (natural vs config_v0_2.MIX_WEIGHTS-weighted) on v0.2.1. 2 seed(s). Decides: G-v1's sampling config + late-curriculum design.*

| condition | general ↓ | authored ↓ | transl. ↓ | authored_v2 ↓ | FLORES ↓ |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| natural | 1.8409 ±0.071 | 2.1987 ±0.081 | 1.7944 ±0.060 | 1.8828 ±0.068 | 2.0834 ±0.035 |
| weighted | 1.8677 ±0.031 | 2.1724 ±0.045 | 1.8201 ±0.022 | 1.8956 ±0.028 | 2.1033 ±0.026 |

**Answer.** Directionally yes but within the seed spread — upweighting nudges authored BPB DOWN (authored Δ=-0.0263) and translationese UP (transl Δ=0.0257), exactly the intended register shift, but every delta is smaller than the ~0.07 two-seed spread and general BPB rises slightly (Δ=0.0268). The authored pool (~6% of tokens) is too small to move voice much by reweighting alone.

**Decision.** G-v1 uses NATURAL sampling for the main run (the weights don't clear the noise floor and cost a little general BPB); reserve config_v0_2.MIX_WEIGHTS for an optional late-curriculum tail, and pursue authored voice mainly via more authored DATA.

### Q7 — Corpus v0.1 vs v0.2.1 — does fineweb-2's bulk help or dilute?

*Setup: corpus (v0.1 vs v0.2.1); same tokenizer, fixed compute (381 steps). 2 seed(s). Decides: whether G-v1 trains on v0.2.1 at all (biggest decision in the fleet).*

| condition | general ↓ | authored ↓ | transl. ↓ | authored_v2 ↓ | FLORES ↓ |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| v0.1 | 1.8592 ±0.054 | 2.1793 ±0.064 | 1.8225 ±0.045 | 1.9679 ±0.061 | 2.1768 ±0.118 |
| v0.2.1 | 1.8409 ±0.071 | 2.1987 ±0.081 | 1.7944 ±0.060 | 1.8828 ±0.068 | 2.0834 ±0.035 |

**Answer.** Helps — v0.2.1 never loses and wins clearly where it counts: **FLORES Δ=-0.0934** and the authored-journalism axis **authored_v2 Δ=-0.0851** are both well beyond the ~0.07 seed spread, with general (Δ=-0.0183) and translation (Δ=-0.0281) also favouring v0.2.1 within noise. fineweb-2's bulk + the VOA/register tail add signal, not dilution.

**Decision.** G-v1 trains on v0.2.1 (the biggest fleet decision — a clean win on FLORES + the journalism axis).

### Q3 — Junk filtering — does v0.1's de-junk win at fixed compute?

*Setup: corpus (v0 vs v0.1); same tokenizer, fixed compute. 1 seed(s). Decides: how aggressive E's future filters should get.*

| condition | general ↓ | authored ↓ | transl. ↓ | authored_v2 ↓ | FLORES ↓ |

### Q5 — Epoch stretch — how far does repetition stretch our corpus?

*Setup: 4 vs 8 vs 12 epochs of a fixed 25M-token unique subsample. 1 seed(s). Decides: G-v1's token schedule; re-validates the Muennighoff repetition band at our scale.*

| condition | general ↓ | authored ↓ | transl. ↓ | authored_v2 ↓ | FLORES ↓ |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 4ep | 2.1607 | 2.6050 | 2.0590 | 2.2170 | 2.2597 |
| 8ep | 1.7909 | 2.1342 | 1.7522 | 1.8303 | 2.0761 |
| 12ep | 1.6293 | 1.9398 | 1.6161 | 1.6572 | 1.9845 |

**Answer.** BPB 2.1607→1.7909→1.6293 at 4→8→12 epochs (Δ4→8=-0.3698, Δ8→12=-0.1616); best at **12ep**.

**Decision.** Still improving at 12 epochs — G-v1 can safely repeat further; extend the token schedule.

## Recommended G-v1 config

- **Corpus:** v0.2.1  *(Q7)*
- **Tokenizer:** kreyol-bpe (Q1-confirmed)
- **Depth:** d12 — d12 1.6891 vs d16 1.7036 general BPB on v0.2.1 (~175M tok) — d12 still wins  *(Part 3)*
- **Mix:** natural sampling (mix weights did not beat natural at fleet scale)  *(Q2)*
- **Epochs / token budget:** ≈4–6 epochs (~0.9–1.3B effective tokens on v0.2.1's ~216M unique). Q5 shows BPB still falling at 12× repetition — no overfit ceiling hit, so this is a safe floor, extensible if compute allows (G v0's 6.7 epochs sits inside this band).  *(Q5)*
